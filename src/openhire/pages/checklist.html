求职清单

找工作的七步。每一步写明谁做、去哪里做。全部免费，不用注册。这页打不开时，用国内镜像。最后更新 2026-11-03。

已读不回？先看岗位还在不在。OpenHire 让 AI 助手直接从雇主自己的系统里替你找工作，简历不出你的电脑。

有几步要你自己做。这页全部列出。收藏这页：网址变了、骗局变了，这页跟着改，改动记在页尾。

定方向

  • 写下你的条件

    写下目标城市、岗位方向、个人贡献者还是带团队、年限、三到五个技能、不考虑的方向。贴给助手。

    在助手里选「求职清单」提示词。它按这页带你走。还没接好 OpenHire？看连接向导。

    谁做：你

搜岗位

  • 搜在架岗位

    让助手调 search_jobs。每条带公司、地点、雇主系统里的原始发布日期、在架天数、投递链接。HR 和招聘岗用分类 hr。

    谁做：OpenHire。在架时间长的岗位只报天数，不判断它招不招。

  • 看读不到的公司

    岗位放在飞书、BOSS 直聘或没有列表的官网上时，打开读不到的招聘门户。点公司自己的招聘页。助手里用 list_portals。

    谁做：OpenHire 给链接，你打开

  • 加一家不在索引里的公司

    把它的招聘页网址给助手。read_board 现读，add_employer 加进你电脑上的索引。

    谁做：OpenHire

查公司

只要这一步时，用单独一页：查公司。

  • 让助手先查一遍

    让助手调 check_employer。它给一张查证清单：在架数、在架天数、域名注册日期、备案号、骗局字样、制裁名单比对、近期公司事件。每项写明来源和时间。

    谁做：OpenHire。不打分，不下结论。

  • 查工商登记

    在国家企业信用信息公示系统搜公司全称。看成立日期、经营状态、注册资本、法定代表人。也可以用爱企查或天眼查个人免费入口（境外网络不能用）。把结果告诉助手。

    谁做：你。OpenHire 不代查，不存你查了谁。

  • 查官网备案

    在工信部 ICP 备案查询输入官网域名。主办单位必须是这家公司。

    谁做：你

  • 查法院和信用记录

    在中国裁判文书网和信用中国搜全称。

    谁做：你

  • 查香港和境外公司

    香港：公司註冊處電子服務（查公司名称免费）。其他地方：OpenCorporates。

    谁做：你

反诈骗三步

  • ① 看到这些，停下

    如果：

    1. 要你先交钱（培训费、保证金、设备费）

    2. 要你先出境面试或交护照

    3. 包机票的境外高薪

    4. 不要经验的高薪

    5. 打字员、刷单

    停下！

    谁做：OpenHire 标出这些字样（含繁体和香港警方列的骗局特征）。决定由你做。

  • ② 只在雇主自己的系统投递

    只点雇主自己域名或招聘系统的链接。不点群聊、私信里的链接、二维码、表格。

    谁做：OpenHire 只给 https 的雇主自有链接

  • ③ 不确定时，先查

    大陆：用国家反诈中心 App 查电话、网址、收款账户；在网络不良与垃圾信息举报受理中心举报；报警打 110。香港：用警务处防騙視伏器；打防騙易热线 18222。

    谁做：你

投递

  • 自己投

    打开投递链接。自己投。

    谁做：你。OpenHire 不替你投，不收简历。助手比对简历只在你电脑上做。

  • 记下授权

    让助手调 authorize_application。它只记你的授权和匿名指纹。同一岗位只记一次。

    谁做：OpenHire。不发任何东西给雇主。

盯岗

  • 设雷达

    让助手调 watch_intent 登记这个求职意向。以后说「看看我的雷达」，助手调 check_watches，告诉你新出了什么、关了什么。

    谁做：OpenHire。雷达存在你电脑上，用你自己生成的随机指纹。

面试与 offer

  • 查劳动规则

    合同、社保、试用期、工资：打 12333 或看人社部 12333。香港：看劳工处。

    谁做：你

  • 入职前再核一次

    用「查公司」里的网站，查合同上的全称。公司名、合同主体、发工资的主体必须是同一家。

    谁做：你

Job-search checklist: what to do at each step

Seven steps. Each step says who does it and where. Free. No sign-up. If this page is slow, use the mirror in mainland China. Last updated 2026-11-03.

Companies screen you with AI. Screen them back. OpenHire lets an AI assistant find jobs for you in employers' own hiring systems. Your résumé stays on your computer.

You do some steps yourself. This page lists all of them. Bookmark it: when an address or a scam changes, this page changes, and the change log at the bottom records it.

Set the target

  • Write down your target

    Write the city, the role, individual contributor or management, years of experience, three to five skills, and what you rule out. Paste it to the assistant.

    In the assistant, select the "Job-search checklist" prompt. It follows this page. Not connected yet? Read the connect guide.

    Who: you

Search postings

  • Search live postings

    Ask the assistant to call search_jobs. Each result has the company, location, the original posting date from the employer's system, days on the board, and the apply link. For HR and recruiting roles, use the family hr.

    Who: OpenHire. For an old posting, it reports the days only. It does not say if the role is still open.

  • Find companies we cannot read

    If the postings are on Feishu, BOSS Zhipin or a site with no list, open Hiring portals we cannot read. Click the company's own careers page. In the assistant, use list_portals.

    Who: OpenHire gives the link; you open it

  • Add a company that is not in the index

    Give the assistant the address of its careers page. read_board reads it live. add_employer adds it to the index on your computer.

    Who: OpenHire

Check the company

For this step only, use its own page (in Chinese): 查公司.

  • Let the assistant check first

    Ask the assistant to call check_employer. It gives a checklist: live postings, days on the board, domain registration date, ICP record, scam phrases, sanctions match, recent company events. Each item names its source and time.

    Who: OpenHire. No score. No verdict.

  • Check the company register

    Search the full registered name in the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. Read the founding date, status, registered capital and legal representative. You can also use Aiqicha or Tianyancha's free personal service (not from overseas networks). Tell the assistant what you found.

    Who: you. OpenHire does not look companies up for you and does not store whom you checked.

  • Check the ICP filing

    Enter the domain of the official site in the MIIT ICP filing lookup. The filing holder must be this company.

    Who: you

  • Check court and credit records

    Search the full name in China Judgements Online and Credit China.

    Who: you

  • Check companies in Hong Kong and elsewhere

    Hong Kong: Companies Registry e-Services (name search is free). Elsewhere: OpenCorporates.

    Who: you

Three anti-scam checks

  • ① Stop when you see these

    If:

    1. You must pay first (training fee, deposit, equipment fee)

    2. You must interview abroad or hand over your passport first

    3. High pay abroad with flights included

    4. High pay with no experience required

    5. Typist or order-brushing jobs

    Stop.

    Who: OpenHire marks these phrases (with traditional Chinese spellings and the Hong Kong Police scam patterns). You decide.

  • ② Apply only in the employer's own system

    Click only links on the employer's own domain or hiring system. Do not open links, QR codes or forms from group chats or private messages.

    Who: OpenHire gives only https links on the employer's own system

  • ③ Not sure? Check first

    Mainland China: check the phone number, address or payment account in the National Anti-Fraud Center app (search 国家反诈中心 in your app store). Report to the 12321 reporting centre. Police: 110. Hong Kong: use the Police Scameter. Anti-Scam Helpline: 18222.

    Who: you

Apply

  • Apply yourself

    Open the apply link. Apply yourself.

    Who: you. OpenHire does not apply for you and does not take your résumé. The assistant compares your résumé with a posting only on your computer.

  • Record your authorisation

    Ask the assistant to call authorize_application. It records only your authorisation and an anonymous fingerprint, once per posting.

    Who: OpenHire. It sends nothing to the employer.

Watch

  • Set a radar

    Ask the assistant to call watch_intent for this target. Later, say "check my radar". The assistant calls check_watches and tells you what is new and what has closed.

    Who: OpenHire. The radar stays on your computer, under a random fingerprint you make yourself.

Interview and offer

  • Check the labour rules

    Contracts, social insurance, probation, wages: call 12333 or read 12333.gov.cn. Hong Kong: read the Labour Department site.

    Who: you

  • Check again before you start

    Use the sites under "Check the company". Search the full name on the contract. The company, the contracting entity and the payer must be the same company.

    Who: you
