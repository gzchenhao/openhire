OpenHire

已读不回？先看岗位还在不在。公司用 AI 筛你，你也用 AI 筛公司。

让 AI 助手直接从雇主自己的系统里替你找工作，简历不出你的电脑。岗位还在不在，公司真不真，投之前先查一遍。

每条岗位来自雇主招聘页背后的公开接口，带核验时间和原始投递链接。简历和个人信息不经过服务端。

开始

  • 连接向导

    选你用的 AI 助手（ChatGPT、Claude、豆包、DeepSeek、Cursor、腾讯 WorkBuddy、VS Code）。只看它的步骤。

  • 求职清单

    找工作的七步。每一步写明谁做、去哪里做。免费，不用注册，中英文切换。

  • 查公司

    入职前核对公司。只有官方网站，免费。

  • 读不到的招聘门户

    岗位放在飞书、BOSS 直聘或没有列表的官网上的公司。点它们自己的招聘页。

数据

  • 岗位在架时长月报

    按雇主系统里的发布日，统计每家公司的岗位挂了多久。每月更新。

  • numbers.json

    我们引用的每个数字的出处、口径和测量日期。

  • report-data.json

    月报的原始数据。可以自己重算。

其他

  • 给雇主

    让候选人的 AI 助手读到贵公司系统里的岗位。免费，只读，十分钟。

  • 隐私说明

    三条红线，CI 强制：个人信息不过服务端，排序不能买，雇主只为已授权的结果付费。

  • 源码

    全部开源。运行 uvx openhire@latest serve，得到和我们一样的数。

数据有误或想更正贵公司的岗位状态？ 雇主认领入口， 免费，也可以直接要求我们把贵公司从索引中移除。

OpenHire

Companies screen you with AI. Screen them back. Ghosted? Check whether the job is still open first.

Let an AI assistant find jobs for you in employers' own systems. Your résumé stays on your computer. Is the job still open? Is the company real? Check both before you apply.

Each posting comes from the public API behind the employer's careers page, with the time it was verified and the original apply link. Résumés and personal data do not touch the server.

Start

  • Connect guide

    Select your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Doubao, DeepSeek, Cursor, Tencent WorkBuddy, VS Code). See only its steps.

  • Job-search checklist

    Seven steps. Each step says who does it and where. Free. No sign-up. Chinese and English.

  • Check a company (in Chinese)

    Check a company before you join. Official sites only. Free.

  • Hiring portals we cannot read

    Companies that post on Feishu, BOSS Zhipin or a site with no list. Click their own careers pages.

Data

  • Monthly report on how long postings stay up

    How long each company's postings have been up, by the posting date in the employer's system. Updated monthly.

  • numbers.json

    The source, the counting rule and the date of every number we quote.

  • report-data.json

    The raw data behind the report. Recompute it yourself.

More

  • For employers

    Let candidates' AI assistants read the postings in your hiring system. Free. Read-only. Ten minutes.

  • Privacy policy

    Three red lines, enforced in CI: no personal data through the server, ranking cannot be bought, employers pay only for authorised results.

  • Source code

    All open source. Run uvx openhire@latest serve and get the same numbers.

Something wrong, or want to correct the status of your company's postings? Employer claim, free; you can also ask us to remove your company from the index.
