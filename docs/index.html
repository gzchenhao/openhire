一个面向 agent 的招聘协议。岗位数据全部取自雇主自己官网招聘页背后的公开接口， 每条都带核验时间与原始申请链接，可逐条复核。简历与任何个人信息永不经过服务端。

  • 岗位在架时长月报
    按雇主招聘系统里的真实发布日，统计每家公司的岗位挂了多久。点名，可复核，每月更新。
  • numbers.json
    我们对外引用的每一个数字的原始出处，含口径说明与实测日期。
  • report-data.json
    月报页的全部原始数据，可自行重算。
  • 连接向导
    选你用的 AI 助手（ChatGPT、Claude、豆包、DeepSeek、Cursor、腾讯 WorkBuddy、VS Code），只看那一种的接入步骤和一段整段粘贴的话。
  • 求职清单
    找工作每一步该做什么、哪步能让 AI 替你做、查公司和防骗去哪个官网，一页写全，免费，不用注册，中英文可切换。值得收藏：网址会搬家，骗局会翻新，这页跟着改。
  • 查公司
    入职前核一家公司去哪些官方网站，怎么识别冒充用人方的骗局。只有官方网址，免费。
  • 给雇主
    让候选人的 AI 助手直接读到贵公司自己系统里的岗位。免费、只读、十分钟；没有可读招聘系统的雇主有三条路。
  • 读不到的招聘门户
    岗位放在飞书、BOSS 直聘或没有可读列表的官网上的公司：它们自己的招聘页链接，点开直接看。
  • 隐私说明
    三条 CI 强制的红线：PII 不过服务端、排序不可购买、雇主只为已授权结果付费。
  • 源码
    全部开源。uvx openhire@latest serve 可自行跑一遍，得到和我们一样的数。

数据有误或想更正贵公司的岗位状态？ 雇主认领入口， 免费，也可以直接要求我们把贵公司从索引中移除。

OpenHire

Companies screen you with AI. Screen them back. Ghosted? Check whether the job is still open first.

Let an AI assistant find jobs for you straight from employers' own systems, with your résumé never leaving your computer. Is the job still open? Is the company real? Check both before you apply.

A hiring protocol built for agents. Every posting comes from the public API behind the employer's own careers page, with the time it was last verified and the original apply link, so each one can be checked. Résumés and personal data never touch the server.

  • Monthly report on how long postings stay up
    How long each company's postings have been up, by the real posting date in the employer's own hiring system. Named, checkable, updated monthly.
  • numbers.json
    The source of every number we quote, with how it is counted and when it was measured.
  • report-data.json
    All the raw data behind the monthly report, to recompute it yourself.
  • Connect guide
    Pick the AI assistant you use (ChatGPT, Claude, Doubao, DeepSeek, Cursor, Tencent WorkBuddy, VS Code) and see only its setup steps and the one block to paste.
  • Job-search checklist
    What to do at each step of a job search, which steps an AI can do for you, and which official sites to use to check a company and avoid scams. One page, free, no sign-up, in Chinese and English. Worth a bookmark: addresses move and scams change, and the page changes with them.
  • Check a company (in Chinese)
    Which official sites to use to check a company before you join, and how to spot scams that pose as employers. Official addresses only, free.
  • For employers
    Let candidates' AI assistants read the postings in your own hiring system. Free, read-only, ten minutes; three ways in for employers without a readable hiring system.
  • Hiring portals we cannot read
    Companies whose postings are on Feishu, BOSS Zhipin or a careers site with no readable list: links to their own careers pages, to open directly.
  • Privacy policy
    Three red lines enforced in CI: no personal data through the server, ranking cannot be bought, employers pay only for authorised results.
  • Source code
    All open source. Run uvx openhire@latest serve yourself and get the same numbers we do.

Something wrong, or want to correct the status of your company's postings? Employer claim, free; you can also ask us to remove your company from the index.
